About us

A family studio since the film era

For almost 30 years, Shantiram Dhungana (Abeer) and his wife have personally photographed Gauradaha's weddings, families and milestones.

Our story, in three chapters

Chapter 1

The film years

Lalima Photo Studio began in the analog era, when every portrait meant careful lighting, film loading and hours in the darkroom. Those years taught patience and precision.

Chapter 2

Going digital

As cameras went digital, we learned, invested and adapted early, so Gauradaha didn't have to travel far for modern quality.

Chapter 3

Today

From cinematic wedding films to large flex boards and vehicle number plates, the same husband-and-wife team still serves you, now with today's technology.

SD

Shantiram Dhungana

Known to many as Abeer

Shantiram and his wife run Lalima together, every day. When you visit the studio, you deal with the owners, not staff. Many of the families we photograph today are the children of couples whose weddings we covered years ago.

“The tools have changed many times, but our promise hasn't: treat every customer's memory as if it were our own.”

ललिमा (Lalima) is the red-gold glow of dawn and dusk, the best light in photography.
