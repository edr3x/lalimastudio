Questions we're often asked

How do I book a wedding?

Send us your date on WhatsApp or call. We'll confirm availability and plan with you.

How long do wedding photos and videos take?

We'll give you a clear delivery date when you book, based on the package you choose.

Can I get passport photos the same day?

Yes, usually within minutes.

Do you design flex boards or only print them?

Both. Bring your idea and we'll design it.

Do you print vehicle number plates?

Yes. Ask us what documents to bring.

Do you travel outside Gauradaha?

Tell us your venue when you enquire and we'll let you know.
